# Bubble Tax v0.1.0 Preview · Build 9

- MacBook notch support: see the paid service beside the camera and its remaining quota on the other side.
- Click to expand the displayed service. Collapse with the toolbar button, Escape, or a click in another app.
- Paid plans rotate every eight seconds while collapsed; rotation pauses while details are open.
- Turn off “Use MacBook notch” in Settings to use the menu bar. Displays without a notch use the menu bar automatically.
- Existing settings and usage history are preserved.

To update, quit Bubble Tax and replace the app in Applications. Apple silicon · macOS 14+.

Developer ID signed; Apple notarization is not complete. If macOS blocks opening, see [Apple’s instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/open-a-mac-app-from-an-unknown-developer-mh40616/mac).
