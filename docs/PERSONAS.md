# Bubble Tax personas

24 English personas. Classification uses observed unused quota near reset, never current Left alone. One representative quota per provider; account attribution and near-reset evidence are required. Unknown or stale history stays Persona Pending.

Personal bands: 98+ Cloud Conservationist; 90+ Environmentalist; 85+ Ghost Subscriber; 75+ Donation Angel (Premium Philanthropist if every personal price is exact, same USD currency, total >= $100/month and quota history covers every personal plan); 70+ Token Trasher; 60+ Bubble Sponsor (Accidental VC with 3+ plans); 50+ Benchwarmer (Subscription Collector with 2+ plans); 35+ Backup Plan Hoarder (FOMO Collector with 3+ plans); 15+ Token Maxxer; 5+ Rate-Limit Regular; below 5 Quota Menace. Prices from the public catalog remain estimates. Ambiguous tiers, incomplete prices and non-USD currencies cannot trigger the premium-cost persona.

Work-only bands: 98+ Empty Seat; 80+ Corporate Philanthropist; 65+ Procurement Prank; 50+ Budget Bonfire; 20+ Procurement Favorite; below 20 Office Power User. Work costs never enter personal billing totals. Known free-only connections: one Free-Tier Legend, two or more Free-Plan Collector. Unchanged evidence produces the same persona; no arbitrary refresh rotation. Small or partial quota coverage remains marked Early read: any represented plan with fewer than three eligible windows, or incomplete plan coverage. Use the widest reported budget; missing weekly history cannot inherit short-window history. Persona copy describes historical quota patterns, not verified cash loss or a final cancellation verdict.
