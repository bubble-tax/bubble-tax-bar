About the data

The app reads available usage through your signed-in provider sessions, with local Claude / Codex records as fallback. Claude and Codex window history adds local model context. The main screen estimates the daily pattern needed to finish your weekly quota. Usage history and completed-change records stay on your Mac. Missing readings stay unknown; Codex quota doesn't represent every ChatGPT feature.

Prices are published US monthly list prices, not your invoice. Savings are projected rate reductions. You cancel or downgrade with the provider, then record the change here. Work budgets stay out of personal savings.
